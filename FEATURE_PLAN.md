# StratOS Feature Development Plan
*Last Updated: May 25, 2025*

## Product Vision

StratOS is an AI-powered strategic operating system that transforms how CEOs and executive teams plan, analyze, and execute business strategy. By leveraging a multi-agent AI system, StratOS provides personalized, intelligent support throughout the entire strategic management lifecycle.

## Core Value Propositions

1. **AI-Powered Strategic Thinking**: 15 specialized agents provide expert-level analysis and recommendations
2. **Unified Strategic Platform**: Single source of truth for vision, goals, initiatives, and execution
3. **Natural Language Interface**: Conversational AI that understands business context
4. **Real-Time Adaptation**: Continuous learning from user preferences and business outcomes
5. **Visual Strategy Mapping**: Interactive canvas for strategic planning and visualization
